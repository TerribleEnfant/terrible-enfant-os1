Cleanest all-around match. Certified **Nuvemshop** partner (also VTEX/Shopify), Meta + Google paid media (+ Bing/Criteo/Pinterest), Google Partner, **ABCOMM Best Digital Performance Agency 2025**. Only candidate hitting Nuvemshop + SP presence + full paid media + an award at once. Confirmed via Nuvemshop's own partner directory.

**2. Ideia Vertical** — Niterói, RJ (not São Paulo)
Best exact platform match: certified **Nuvemshop NEXT and VTEX**, plus Shopify. 500+ stores since 2013. The hedge if a VTEX move is possible later. Knock: Rio state, not SP city.
https://ideiavertical.com.br/

**3. Cadastra** — São Paulo HQ (900+ staff, 300+ clients, LATAM + EMEA)
Deepest strategic / full-funnel partner. Meta + Google full-funnel, VTEX implementation, CRO, founded 2000 (first Brazilian SEM agency). **Has a Buenos Aires office** — natural ARG↔BRA bridge. Trade-offs: enterprise altitude/pricing; **not Nuvemshop-specific**.
https://en.cadastra.com/

**4. Econverse** — São Paulo
Best luxury-fashion credibility. VTEX (Quadrant 2024) / Wake / Shopify implementation + marketing. Portfolio: **NK Store** (top Brazilian luxury boutique), **BAW Clothing**, Dudah Beauty. Closest to the actual vertical. Does **not** list Nuvemshop.
https://www.econverse.com.br/en

**5. Agência E-Plus** — Barueri (Greater SP / Alphaville)
Solid SP-metro performance + platform shop. VTEX-primary (also Shopify, Wake, deco.cx headless), Google/Meta/TikTok, SEO, CRM automation, remarketing, affiliates. Google Premier Partner + Meta-certified. No Nuvemshop.
https://www.agenciaeplus.com.br/

**6. CAUP** — Salvador, Bahia
Most overtly **AI-forward** verified — markets "Agentes de I.A" as a primary service alongside e-commerce + marketing. Covers VTEX/Shopify/Tray/WooCommerce. Weak on: no SP-city office, no Nuvemshop.
https://caup.com.br/
