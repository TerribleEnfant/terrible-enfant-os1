## 1. Brand identity

**Core concept:** Post-ironic elegance. Latin underground. Minimal decadence.

> "Terrible Enfant is not a brand — it's an aesthetic position. It exists in the tension between discipline and destruction; between couture and chaos. It speaks to those who live beautifully inside contradiction."

**Tagline:** *Elegance born from disobedience.*

**Spirit:** Disobedient sophistication.
**Language:** Existential streetwear meets literary decadence.
**Energy:** Paris + Buenos Aires underground.

**Cultural position — luxury punk aesthetics:**
The brand operates within a "luxury punk" framework where elegance is not obedience. References include Saint Laurent's cathedral and the alley behind it. The shoe is an ontological statement, not an accessory. Fashion as a medium for existential insurrection.

**Philosophical register:** High. Shoe descriptions are written at PhD-adjacent level — citing metaphysics of art, epistemology of culture, radical fashion theory. Key academic references used: continental philosophy, ontological resistance, phenomenological readings of objects. Specifically invoked: the *objet liminal*, the *ente liminar* (liminal entity between body and concept).

**Tone:** Cold. Precise. Editorial. Never generic. Never warm. Never explanatory for a general audience.

**Manifest concept — Estado de Gracia:**
The current identity campaign centers on *Estado de Gracia* (State of Grace). The idea: a suspended moment where one need not justify oneself. No guilt, no debt, no past. An almost indecent freedom. Framed between the spiritual and the carnal, the superficial and the transcendent, virtue and vice.

**Brand as weapon:** If Terrible Enfant were a sword, it would be a *smallsword / estoque ceremonial europeo* — urban, civil, dangerous without announcing it. Injures through precision, not force. Later also mapped as an *estoc* — designed to penetrate armor by reading its joints, not fighting its mass.

## 6. Writing register — what Claude should do

**For product copy (shoe descriptions):**
- Medium-long version: ontological/philosophical take + material/technical specs
- Short version: 2–3 lines, sharp, no adjective inflation
- Technical terms to use: *cuero vacuno de alto brillo*, *costura golf*, *construcción Blake*, *doble suela cosida*, *empeine textil*, *piel exótica*, *costura moc toe*, *cuero grabado*
- Register: between product description and philosophical manifesto

**For campaigns:**
- Conceptual document first (concept + scenes + copy + tone)
- Then brief for collaborators
- Then short copy for posts
- Never explain the concept in the post copy

**For brand writing (manifestos, identity):**
- Continental philosophy register
- Dense, citational when needed
- Spanish preferred for Argentine/internal audience
- English for international collabs and documentation
- Never apologize for obscurity

**For operations (briefs, status reports, PM comms):**
- Clear, direct, minimal
- Brief for Fanny: step-by-step, actionable
- Investor updates: bullish headline → concrete facts → cautious note at the end if needed

**Never do:**
- Generic fashion copy ("timeless elegance," "crafted with care")
- Warm or accessible marketing tone
- Explain the brand's positioning to the reader
- Use emoji in editorial copy
- Flatten philosophical references into plain language
