Execution Scope

What gets
done

Implementation · One-Shot

E-Commerce Build

Full Nuvemshop store build including custom UX/UI design, Figma prototype, native integrations (logistics + payments), all page types (Home, PLP, PDP, My Account, Blog), QA, staging, and Go-Live. 30-day post-launch support included.

Setup: 40 dedicated hours (one-shot) for initial configuration and platform onboarding before operations begin.
Monthly

E-Commerce Operations

Day-to-day platform management: ERP and logistics integrations, catalog management, pricing updates, SEO-optimized product descriptions, banner updates, security setup, tax configuration, and basic CRM automation flows.

Monthly

Tech Evolution

Ongoing platform improvements: UX/UI iterations, A/B testing, CRO analysis, new feature development, and performance monitoring.

Recommended: 5h/month at R$ 250/h. Scale as needed.
Monthly

Paid Media

Full-funnel campaign management across Meta, Google, and relevant channels. Strategic planning, editorial calendar, daily budget control, performance copywriting, and monthly reporting.

To properly calibrate campaigns, we need Terrible Enfant's monthly sales targets defined in advance — ideally month-by-month for the first 12 months.
Optional

SEO & Generative AI Search

Full SEO audit, keyword research, technical optimization, schema markup, AI-powered content strategy (GAI), and monthly performance reporting with organic traffic benchmarks.

Optional · From Month 4

Inbound Marketing & CRM

CRM setup, audience segmentation, automated workflow creation, lead scoring, and email/WhatsApp marketing dispatches.

We recommend starting no earlier than Month 4 — the first three months are dedicated to building the store and collecting an initial customer base. Launching CRM without an audience produces little return.
