# Gemini Prompts — Los Siete (visualización con estilista)

**Propósito:** generar 7 imágenes preliminares para discutir looks con el estilista.
**Estado:** v2 — setting unificado a interiores wood-paneled mid-century.
**Engine:** Gemini Imagen 4 (o equivalente). Formato vertical 4:5.

> Estas imágenes **no son styling final**. Son punto de arranque para conversación. El styling definitivo lo decide Comando + estilista a partir de estas imágenes como base.

---

## Cómo usar

1. Copiar el **Master Prompt Block** al inicio del prompt.
2. Pegar la sección específica del arquetipo debajo.
3. Generar 2–3 variaciones por arquetipo para tener opciones.
4. Repetir hasta que la dirección estética sea consistente entre los 7.
5. Llevar las imágenes seleccionadas al estilista como base de la sesión.

Los prompts están en inglés porque los modelos de imagen funcionan mejor con prompts técnicos en inglés. Las notas para vos / estilista están en español.

---

## Decisión de locación (v2)

Los siete arquetipos viven en el **mismo tipo de espacio**: interior wood-paneled mid-century — paredes de planchas verticales de madera, tonos cálidos (caoba, nogal, teak), generalmente vacío, con piso de alfombra suave o madera. Detalles arquitectónicos contenidos: una luz cenital empotrada, una silla mid-century, una puerta abierta hacia un pasillo del mismo material. Estética office-elevated, contenida, casi institucional.

La unidad de locación refuerza la lectura conceptual — **siete versiones de la misma persona en el mismo mundo**. Lo que diferencia a cada arquetipo es la **luz, el framing, el wardrobe y la actitud corporal**, nunca el lugar.

Referentes visuales: editoriales tipo Bottega Veneta SS22, late-90s Jil Sander campaigns, Helmut Lang office editorials, Roy Andersson interiors, Mad Men cinematography aplicado a mood luxury punk.

---

## Moodboard de referencia (wardrobe + actitud)

Estética luxury punk en la línea de Helmut Lang fin de los 90, Enfants Riches Déprimés, Hedi Slimane en Saint Laurent, Maison Margiela tailoring deconstruido. Mezcla con iconografía religiosa degradada (rosarios, crucifijos, prayer beads), botas con herrajes plateados, cuero negro hecho a mano, sastrería oversized, scarves, libros, cigarrillos. Modelo único: andrógino, joven, mirada intensa, manos tatuadas.

## MASTER PROMPT BLOCK

*(pegar al inicio de cada prompt — define el estilo y la locación común que mantiene los 7 unidos)*

```
Editorial fashion photography, shot on 35mm film, visible film grain, natural
cinematic lighting, shallow depth of field, muted desaturated palette, luxury
punk aesthetic in the lineage of late-1990s Helmut Lang, Enfants Riches
Déprimés, late Hedi Slimane Saint Laurent, and Maison Margiela artisanal.

LOCATION (common to all images): interior of a mid-century wood-paneled
room. Walls made of vertical wooden planks in warm tones — mahogany, walnut,
or teak. The room is mostly empty, contained, almost institutional —
somewhere between a 1960s executive office and a private gallery. The floor
is either soft carpet (deep red, charcoal, or rust) or polished wood.
Architectural details are restrained: a recessed ceiling light, a single
mid-century chair, an open doorway to a corridor of the same material. No
decoration. The wood is the entire vocabulary of the space. Editorial mood
in the lineage of Bottega Veneta SS22, late-90s Jil Sander office
editorials, Helmut Lang campaigns, Roy Andersson interiors.

Single male model, same across all seven images — androgynous, late twenties,
dark wavy hair falling over forehead, lean build, pale skin, intense direct
gaze, subtle hand tattoos visible at wrists. Hair is unstyled or worn back
with calculated negligence.

Wardrobe direction: hand-distressed black leather, oversized tailoring,
silver hardware (harnesses, buckles, studs, signet rings), religious
accessories used as objects not symbols (rosary chains, inverted crucifix,
prayer beads as bracelet), pointed Cuban-heel boots or harness boots with
metal reinforcements, deconstructed suiting with frayed cuffs. Layers worn
with intentional asymmetry.

Vertical 4:5 aspect ratio. Photographic realism — no CGI smoothness, no
illustrated quality. No text, no logos, no graphic overlays in the image.
Skin texture, fabric texture, and wood grain visible at high detail.
```

## I — El Hereje

> El que conoce el dogma de memoria. Eligió irse.

```
Setting variation: wood-paneled corner of the common room. A single thin
shaft of cold light enters from off-frame, hitting one wall sharply, leaving
the rest in shadow. A small object out of place on the wood: a wooden
crucifix turned face-to-wall, or a row of unlit votive candles on the
baseboard.

Pose: medium shot, three-quarter angle to camera. Hands held in residual
liturgical gesture — open, palms facing inward — but holding nothing. Gaze
directed past the camera, slightly downward, expression of someone
mid-thought, not posing.

Wardrobe: long black wool overcoat with priest-like cut, high collar. White
collarless shirt unbuttoned at neck. Inverted silver crucifix worn on a
leather cord. Black wool trousers. Pointed black leather boots with metal
toe caps. No watch, no rings.

Light: cold lateral light from off-frame, deep shadows on the opposite
side of the face and on the wood behind him. The wood reads almost black
in the shadow zone, glowing warm where the light hits.

Palette: dirty ivory, deep black, cold light cutting through warm wood.

Mood: someone who knows the entire creed by memory and refuses to recite it.
Composure of the seminary, none of the belief. The room is too clean for
his state of mind.
```

## II — El Aristócrata Desheredado

> Cortesía sin patrimonio. Modales sin reino.

```
Setting variation: the wood-paneled room with a single mid-century
upholstered armchair (worn velvet or cracked leather) slightly off-frame to
one side. Late afternoon amber light entering through an unseen window
warms the wood. The room reads as a private waiting area in a building that
no longer functions.

Pose: American shot (mid-thigh up). Standing, one hand resting lightly on
the back of the chair, weight on one leg. Slight inclination of the head as
if greeting someone he doesn't quite recognize. Gaze direct but tired.

Wardrobe: oversized double-breasted blazer in faded camel wool, slightly
too large at the shoulders. White cotton shirt with frayed cuffs, top
button open. Dark grey wool trousers with broken crease. Polished black
leather derby shoes with visible patina. Silk pocket square the color of
old blood. Single gold signet ring on the little finger.

Light: warm amber from window-right, soft shadow falling left. Dust
particles visible in the light beam. The wood glows.

Palette: sepia, faded camel, ox-blood, warm wood, old leather.

Mood: someone offering you a seat in a house that no longer belongs to
him. Manners as the last remaining piece of inheritance. The room knows
him too well.
```

## III — El Confesor

> Escucha. No habla. Sabe todo.

```
Setting variation: tight, narrower wood-paneled corner of the room — the
camera reads the panels as confessional walls without literally being a
confessional. The wood almost completely fills the frame behind him. No
furniture visible.

Pose: extreme close-up of face, neck, and upper shoulders. Body oriented
slightly toward camera as if leaning in to listen. Head tilted barely
forward. Eyes fixed on camera, lips closed, expression of patient,
weightless attention.

Wardrobe: high-collared black wool coat, no visible jewelry, no
embellishment. The face, the fabric, and the wood are the entire image.

Light: harsh single sidelight from camera-left, casting half the face in
deep shadow. The lit side reveals high skin and fabric texture. The wood
behind him reads in low warm tones, slightly out of focus.

Palette: warm dark wood, skin, deep black coat. No other color.

Mood: someone who could ruin any person in the room and chooses not to.
The elegance of withheld power. The wood is the booth, but he is the
priest, not the penitent.
```

## IV — El Mártir Voluntario

> Eligió el fuego. Lo eligió bien vestido.

```
Setting variation: the wood-paneled room from a wider angle — model
standing dead-center on a deep red carpet, the walls receding equally on
both sides. The space functions as a stage. Open doorway behind him leads
to the same corridor of the same wood. Symmetrical.

Pose: full-body shot, slightly low camera angle. Standing with arms
relaxed at his sides, facing camera directly. Posture of a fighter in the
moment before — disciplined, prepared, no tension in the face but
unmistakable tension in the body. Slight outward turn of the feet.

Wardrobe: black leather harness worn directly over a thin white sleeveless
tank, exposing the shoulders. Slim black leather pants with no belt. Black
ankle boots with low heel and silver side buckle. Hair slicked straight
back, severe. No accessories — the body is the accessory.

Light: hard cenital light from a recessed ceiling fixture directly above
him, creating deep eye shadows, a halo on the top of the hair, and a sharp
shadow under the chin. The light falls into a near-perfect pool on the red
carpet around his feet.

Palette: deep red carpet, warm dark wood walls, off-white tank, black
leather, carbon-cold skin.

Mood: Mishima the morning of the act. The room becomes the ritual space.
The strategist of his own sacrifice, framed by architecture.
```

## V — El Verdugo

> Belleza que no tiembla.

```
Setting variation: a tighter section of the same wood-paneled room — the
camera reads only torso and below, framed by vertical wood planks on both
sides. The wood looks darker here, almost ceremonial. Floor in polished
dark wood, no carpet.

Pose: cropped portrait from sternum to mid-thigh. Face out of frame (or
hidden behind a black silk mask if the framing must include the head).
One gloved hand holding a worn object of trade (vintage straight razor,
shoe last, or small leather tool — choose the most charged). Other hand at
side. Body in slight profile. No movement.

Wardrobe: long black leather coat falling past the hip, buttoned. Black
leather gloves, well-worn. Harness boots with silver D-ring buckles and
visible scuffs. Long black leather apron over the coat, frayed at the hem.

Light: direct hard light from above, almost no soft shadow. The wood
behind reflects very little — almost black. Quality of light slightly cold
in temperature, contrasting the warm wood.

Palette: matte black, steel grey, dark warm wood, hint of leather brown.

Mood: precision without morality. Beauty as lethal exactness. The
elegance of someone who does what must be done. The room offers him no
witnesses.
```

## VI — El Espectro

> Sobrevivió a algo. No dice a qué.

```
Setting variation: the wood-paneled room reads as a corridor — the camera
catches an open doorway behind him, leading to another identical
wood-paneled passage receding into shadow. The repetition creates the
sense of a building that goes on forever. Polished wood floor reflects
him faintly.

Pose: medium-long shot. Figure standing slightly off-center, one hand
against the wood paneling, the other holding a leather-bound book at his
side. Gaze unfocused, into middle distance, past the camera. Slight motion
blur on the face — or a faint double exposure where the face is almost
duplicated, as if two presences occupying one body.

Wardrobe: oversized grey wool overcoat, almost trench-length. Charcoal
wool trousers. No visible shirt — only the coat and the body. Black
ankle boots, quiet. No jewelry. The leather book is the only personal
object.

Light: pale, cool, almost monochromatic — daylight filtering from an
unseen source at the end of the corridor. The warm wood drops to grey
under this light. No warmth survives.

Palette: cooled-down wood (almost grey), bone white, faint dusty rose in
the skin.

Mood: someone who already crossed over once and came back for reasons of
his own. The room cools when he enters it. The corridor behind him knows
where he was.
```

## VII — El Niño Terrible

> El epónimo. Furia con buenos modales.

```
Setting variation: the wood-paneled room at its cleanest — centered,
symmetrical, no furniture, soft carpet in muted color. Camera at eye
level. The room reads as the model's own portrait studio. This is the
canonical version of the location.

Pose: full frontal direct portrait, three-quarter length (knees up).
Hands at sides, slight forward lean of the chest. Minimal smile — barely
a movement at the corner of the mouth, the smile of someone who
understood before anyone else. Direct eye contact with the camera, no
submission.

Wardrobe: tailored black wool jacket, slightly oversized, single-breasted,
notch lapel. White cotton shirt half-untucked, top two buttons open. Slim
black leather pants. Pointed Cuban-heel boots in polished black leather.
Multiple chunky silver rings on both hands. Faded indigo neckerchief worn
high around the neck. Visible hand tattoos.

Light: clean even light from a recessed ceiling fixture, with one slight
directional shadow falling forward. Slightly warm, photographic. The wood
glows but never overpowers.

Palette: warm wood, paper white, carbon black, silver, skin.

Mood: the brand's mirror. Fury with good manners. The face that looks
back when la marca looks at itself. The room is his.
```

## Notas operativas para Comando + estilista

- **Locación única:** el espacio es siempre el mismo tipo de interior — wood-paneled mid-century. Lo que cambia entre arquetipos es **el ángulo, la luz, los objetos puntuales en escena, y el wardrobe**. Si Gemini introduce paredes blancas, cemento, exteriores, o styling que rompa con la madera, regenerar.

- **Modelo único:** el modelo es la misma persona en los 7 prompts. Si Gemini lo cambia mucho entre generaciones, agregar al master prompt una descripción más específica del rostro (color de ojos, forma de mandíbula, peinado exacto) o usar reference image upload si la versión de Gemini lo soporta.

- **Coherencia entre los 7:** el master prompt block (incluyendo la sección LOCATION) es lo que mantiene la unidad. **No cambiarlo entre arquetipos** — solo cambia el bloque específico de Setting variation, pose, wardrobe, luz, paleta, mood.

- **Tatuajes en las manos:** importantes para legibilidad de "Niño Terrible" y para la consistencia entre arquetipos. Si Gemini los hace inconsistentes, especificar diseño exacto (cruz pequeña en el pulgar, número romano en el índice, etc.).

- **Deformación AI viene después:** estos prompts producen el rostro base. La deformación AI por arquetipo (capa 03 del método de producción) se aplica en post sobre las fotos reales del shoot — no en estos previews.

- **Religious imagery filters:** Gemini puede tener filtros para crucifijos invertidos, rosarios, etc. Si rechaza un prompt, reemplazar "inverted crucifix" por "antique pendant" y ajustar después manualmente con el estilista.

- **Wood grain consistency:** especificar en cada generación el tipo de madera (mahogany / walnut / teak) si Gemini empieza a mezclar maderas claras y oscuras. La consistencia del material define la lectura de "mismo mundo".

- **Iteración esperada:** 2–3 rondas de prompts antes de tener una base usable. La primera ronda calibra el rostro, el styling, y la locación. La segunda afina mood por arquetipo. La tercera ajusta detalles finos.
